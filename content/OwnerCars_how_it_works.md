# How OwnerCars works

Selling or buying a car privately shouldn't mean handing your personal details to strangers. OwnerCars is built so you stay in control — your contact details, your registration, and your documents are never exposed without your say-so. Here's how it works.

---

## Messaging — everything happens on the platform

On most sites, the moment you advertise a car your phone number and email are out in the open. On OwnerCars, they never are. Buyers and sellers communicate entirely through OwnerCars' own secure messaging.

- A buyer enquiring about a car sends a message through the advert — no phone number or email changes hands.
- The seller replies through the same thread.
- Your contact details, your exact location, and your registration number stay private throughout.

This means no scam texts, no nuisance calls, and no details leaking the moment you respond to an enquiry.

## You need an account to make contact

To message a seller, a buyer needs an OwnerCars account. This is deliberate, and it protects everyone:

- It keeps conversations tied to real, contactable accounts rather than anonymous one-off messages.
- It means sellers aren't fielding messages from throwaway addresses.
- It keeps the whole exchange on the platform, where your privacy is protected.

Creating an account takes moments, and a buyer's details are never shown to the seller — only their messages.

## You're alerted by email

You don't need to sit watching the site. OwnerCars emails you when something happens:

- **Sellers** are emailed when a buyer sends a first message about their car, and when a buyer replies in an existing conversation.
- **Buyers** are emailed when the seller replies to them.
- When a buyer requests one of your Secure Vault documents, you're emailed about that too.
- The email lets you know there's a message waiting and links you back to the conversation on OwnerCars — the message itself stays on the platform.

So you'll never miss an enquiry, but the conversation always stays in the secure environment where it belongs.

## The Secure Vault — share documents on your terms

This is one of the most powerful parts of OwnerCars. Selling a car often means sharing sensitive paperwork — the V5C logbook, MOT certificate, service history. Hand these to the wrong "buyer" and you're giving away personal and vehicle data that can be misused.

The Secure Vault lets you share these documents safely, and only when you choose to.

**How it works for sellers:**

- In your advert, you can upload documents to your Secure Vault: your MOT certificate, service history, a redacted V5C, and a video link.
- These documents are stored privately. They are never shown publicly on your advert.
- When a buyer is interested and asks to see a document, you receive the request in your messages — and decide whether to share it.
- If you choose to share, the buyer receives a secure, time-limited link that expires after 15 minutes. You stay in control of who sees what, and when.

**How it works for buyers:**

- Once you're in a conversation with a seller, you can request to see a document from their Secure Vault.
- The seller decides whether to share it. If they do, you'll get a secure link to view it on OwnerCars.
- This protects the seller — and means the documents you're shown are shared deliberately, not scattered around the internet.

You decide who sees your paperwork, one document at a time, and nothing is ever left exposed.

## Your privacy, by design

Everything about OwnerCars is built around keeping the seller in control:

- **Contact details** — never shown. All communication is through the platform.
- **Registration number** — we never display your registration on your advert as a listed detail, so it can't be read off the page or used to clone your vehicle's identity. Your photos are your own, so we'd always recommend obscuring the number plate in any pictures you upload.
- **Location** — shown only as the nearest town, never your full postcode or address.
- **Documents** — shared only through the Secure Vault, on your terms, via time-limited links.

Selling your car shouldn't cost you your privacy. With OwnerCars, it doesn't.
